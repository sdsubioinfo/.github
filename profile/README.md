Welcome to the GitHub organization for the **Bioinformatics & Computational Biology Group** at the **Animal Disease Research and Diagnostic Laboratory (ADRDL)**, Department of Veterinary and Biomedical Sciences, South Dakota State University.

We develop computational tools, genomic pipelines, and machine learning models to support veterinary infectious disease surveillance and diagnostic research.

---

## 🔬 Core Focus Areas

- **Viral Genomics & Omics**
- **Host-Pathogen Interactions**
- **Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence**
- **Tool & Web Server Development**
